Artist Statement

Placeholder content: the quotation below is by Erin Hanson and is repeated three times for layout testing. It will be replaced with Chrissa Shamberger’s artist statement.

“My goal is to capture the beauty of light during the golden hour and transform ordinary landscapes into extraordinary mosaics of color and texture. I apply oil paints in thick, expressive brushstrokes laid side-by-side, without blending... My goal for each painting is to capture a moment in time so beautiful it makes you catch your breath when you see it. I want my paintings to speak to you again and again, drawing you in, igniting a sense of joy every time you look at them.”

— Erin Hanson
