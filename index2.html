About Me

I create experiences that make ideas clear and turn exploration into an adventure. My work brings together learning design, interactive media, art, technology, and storytelling. With a master’s degree in library and information science, I bring a librarian’s eye for organizing information so people can find their way through it. I enjoy crafting engaging ways for people to discover new ideas and develop new skills. I design with the audience in mind, making room for curiosity, play, and imagination.
